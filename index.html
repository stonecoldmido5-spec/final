Travel Logistics

  • Total distance: ~18 km by private van + 2.5 km walking
  • Walking difficulty: Light — flat workshop floors, some outdoor yards
  • Drive time: ~35 minutes spread across the day
  • Best season: October–April (cooler, less humidity for finishing rooms)

Insider Tips

  • Wear: Closed shoes (wood shavings), cotton clothes (finishing rooms are warm)
  • Avoid brokers: Stick to your Damiture Tourism guide — no simsar, no hidden fees
  • Cash: Small workshops prefer Egyptian pounds for tips and small purchases
  • Photos: Always ask before photographing faces — workshop interiors are usually welcome

A city that smells of cedar at dawn.

Where the Nile meets the Mediterranean, an entire city turns wood into beauty. For three centuries, Damietta's families have passed down lathe, chisel and inlay — today producing more than 60% of Egypt's furniture.

From the narrow alley workshops of Suq Abdel Rahman to the modern production halls of Shata Furniture City, Damietta is not just a manufacturing hub. It is a living museum of craft.

Damietta Corniche

✓ Verified

The Nile promenade at sunset, where the air smells of grilled corn and the call to prayer mixes with boat horns. Walk, sit, breathe.

⛵

Felucca Rides

✓ Verified

Sail the Damietta branch of the Nile on a traditional wooden felucca. Captain Magdy has been navigating these waters for 40 years.

🏖️

Ras El-Bar Beach

✓ Verified

Where the Nile meets the Mediterranean. Calm waters, fresh fish grilled on the shore, and the best sunset in the Delta.

🕌

El-Tabia Mosque

✓ Verified

A 19th-century Ottoman mosque with intricate woodwork — ironically, carved by Damietta artisans. The minbar is a masterpiece.

🏮

Suq El-Balah (Old Market)

✓ Verified

The oldest market in Damietta. Spices, textiles, copper pots, and the chaos that makes Egyptian souks unforgettable.

🦐

El-Fanar Restaurant

✓ Verified

Grilled tilapia, sayadeya rice, and the best view of the Nile. The fish arrives fresh from the boat at dawn.

☕

El-Limby Cafe

✓ Verified

Traditional ahwa on the Nile. Mint tea, shisha, and old men playing backgammon. The Damietta that tourists rarely see.

🦢

Lake Manzala

✓ Verified

One of Egypt's largest lakes. Flamingos, herons, and the famous Manzala tilapia. Birdwatching boats available at sunrise.

🧵

Textile Souk

✓ Verified

Egyptian cotton, hand-woven scarves, and the famous Damietta lace. Prices that make Cairo shoppers weep with envy.

🍯

Damietta Sweets

✓ Verified

Basbousa, kunafa, and the famous "mushabak" — honey-drenched pastries that have been made here since Ottoman times.
